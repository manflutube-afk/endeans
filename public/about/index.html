The barbers

Darren & Stella Endean

Endean's is a traditional & modern gents' hairdressers on Harbour Road in Par, run together by husband-and-wife team Darren and Stella Endean. It's a proper local barber shop — you're not a booking slot, you're whoever walked through the door.

In June 2020 Darren was examined and accredited by the British Master Barbers Alliance as an official British Master Barber — a standard that looks hard at barbering skill, professionalism, hygiene and how a shop is run. There aren't many of them in Cornwall.

The shop is a relaxed one: coffee on the side, telly on the wall, free Wi-Fi, and an autism-friendly welcome for anyone who finds a haircut hard going. Take your time — nobody's rushing you out.
