What we do

The Services

Everything from a quick tidy-up to a proper hot-towel wet shave. Straightforward gents' barbering done well — and no appointment needed for any of it. Darren and Stella both work the chairs, and the couple of things Darren looks after on his own are marked below.

Hair Cuts

Clipper work, scissor work or a bit of both. Tell Darren or Stella what you're after, or leave it to them.

Re-styles

Fancy a change? A proper consultation first, then a cut that actually suits your head and your hair.

Wash, Cut & Finish

The full sit-down: shampoo at the basin, cut, then styled and finished with product.

Wet Shaves

Hot towel, warm lather and a traditional razor. The closest shave you'll get anywhere.

Beard Trims

Shaped, tidied and lined out — from a light neaten-up to a full reshape.

Head Shaves

Clean, smooth and comfortable, finished with a hot towel and balm.

Crew Cuts

Sharp, simple and quick. In, done, out the door.

Boys & OAPs

Little ones and older gents looked after, with a bit of patience and a friendlier rate.

Colouring & Highlights

Grey blending, full colour or highlights — subtle or bold, your call.

Darren only

Perms

From a soft wave to a tighter curl, with the aftercare advice to keep it sitting right.

Darren only

Ear Piercing

Done properly and hygienically in the chair, with aftercare advice included.

Darren only

Product Sales

Clays, pastes, pomades and beard oils — the same kit we use on you, to take home.
