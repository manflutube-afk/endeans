Leaving a review takes a minute

Reviews are how most people in Par and St Blazey find the shop in the first place, so they genuinely help. If you have a Google account on your phone, it is three taps.

  1. 1

    Open the shop on Google

    Tap the button above, or search Endean's Barber Shop Par on your phone.

  2. 2

    Choose your stars

    Scroll to the reviews and tap Write a review, then pick one to five stars.

  3. 3

    Say what you thought

    A line or two is plenty. Mentioning the cut you had helps the next person know what to ask for.
